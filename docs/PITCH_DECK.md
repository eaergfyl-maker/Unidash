## Slide 2: 01 - The Real Campus Pain
- **01: The Crunch**
  - 15–30 minute gap between classes, but canteen queues take 25 minutes. Students either skip lunch or arrive late to class.
- **02: The Chaos**
  - Sudden replacement classes or meetings destroy pre-planned schedules.

---

## Slide 3: 02 - The Problem is Documented
- **Key Metric:** **52.46%** of 122 Malaysian undergraduates surveyed skipped meals.
- **Why Students Skip:**
  - Common reasons reported in earlier Malaysian studies, as cited in the same paper: **insufficient time**.
- **Our Solution:**
  - Time pressure is the constraint SyncFlow targets: short gaps between classes and long canteen queues.
- *Source: Int. J. Environ. Res. Public Health 2021, 18(23), 12820 (open access via PubMed Central, PMC8657465). Small survey of Malaysian undergraduates during COVID-19 online learning; indicative, not campus-specific.*

---

## Slide 4: 03 - Why Existing Food Apps Fail on Campus
- **The Parallel Ordering Trap:**
  - Ordering a meal (14 min prep) along with iced coffee (3 min prep) simultaneously.
- **Dilemma 1:**
  - Wait in 2 separate physical lines during lecture hours.
- **Dilemma 2:**
  - Iced coffee melts and gets watered down while waiting for food.

---

## Slide 5: 04 - Example of How SyncFlow Works
- **Without SyncFlow: Both Ordered at Once**
  - **Meal:** 14 min prep
  - **Iced Coffee:** 3 min prep $\rightarrow$ Ready at minute 3, waits 11 min: ice melts!
  - *Coffee is ready at minute 3 and sits for 11 minutes before the meal finishes.*
- **With SyncFlow: Staggered Kitchen Tickets**
  - **Meal:** 14 min prep
  - **Iced Coffee:** Drink ticket held until minute 11 $\rightarrow$ Coffee brews in 3 min.
  - *Meal and coffee are both ready together at minute 14.*

---

## Slide 6: 05 - Enter SyncFlow: The Secret Sauce
1. **Global Multi-Shop Cart:**
   - Order from different stalls with 1 single checkout option.
2. **Sync-Fire JIT Engine:**
   - Staggers kitchen tickets so the drink brews at the exact minute the food finishes.
3. **Class-to-Counter:**
   - Upload timetable (PDF / screenshot) and AI detects the break gaps and flags risky stalls.
